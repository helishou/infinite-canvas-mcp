Original, unmodified WOFF2 unicode subsets are bundled by Vite with the application.
Weights 100–900, normal style. The browser requests subsets needed for displayed
characters; no font CDN or blanket preload is required. Characters outside the
package coverage fall back to the declared platform fonts.
